❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete my account? +
Go to Settings → Delete Account. If you can't access the app, email us at appcreator001.harshitkumar@gmail.com with your username.
How do I report inappropriate content? +
Tap the three dots (⋯) on any video or comment and select "Report". Our team will review it within 24 hours.
Can I get my data back after deletion? +
No. Account deletion is permanent. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.
How do I make my account private? +
Go to Settings → Private Account → Toggle ON. Only approved followers will see your videos.
Someone is harassing me. What should I do? +
Block the user immediately (Profile → Block). Then report them via email with screenshots. We take harassment very seriously.
Why was my video removed? +
Your video may have violated our Terms of Service (e.g., nudity, violence, copyright). Check your email for details.
How do I change my username? +
Go to Profile → Edit Profile → Change User ID. Make sure it's unique.
Is ReelHub free to use? +
Yes! ReelHub is completely free. We may add premium features in the future.
