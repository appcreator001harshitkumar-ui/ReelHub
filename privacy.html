Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 23 September 2026

🔒 Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Introduction

Welcome to ReelHub ("we", "our", "us"). ReelHub is a social video-sharing platform that allows users to watch, upload, and share short videos. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our app.

By using ReelHub, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our app.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile photo from Google Sign-In.
  • Profile Details: Username, age, gender, bio (optional).
  • Content: Videos, photos, and messages you upload or send.
  • Comments: Text comments you post on videos.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Browser type, device type, operating system.
  • Usage Data: Videos watched, likes, comments, shares, follows.
  • IP Address: For security and analytics.
  • Cookies: For maintaining your login session.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Google Account: When you sign in with Google, we receive your basic profile info (name, email, photo).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the ReelHub app.
  • Enable video sharing, comments, and messaging features.
  • Show your profile to other users.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and spam.
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide support.
  • Send notifications about activity on your account.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. How We Store Your Data

Your data is stored securely using:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, and notifications.
  • Cloudinary: Secure storage for videos and images.
  • GitHub Pages: App hosting.

All data is transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted). We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal data.

We may share limited information with:

  • Service Providers: Firebase, Cloudinary (for storage and functionality).
  • Law Enforcement: When required by law, court order, or to protect safety.
  • Other Users: Your public profile, videos, and comments are visible to others.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data.
  • Correction: Update or correct your information.
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data.
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw Consent: Stop using our services at any time.

To exercise these rights, visit our Account Deletion page or contact us.

7. Children's Privacy

ReelHub is not intended for children under 13 years. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we discover such data, we will delete it immediately.

Users between 13-18 must have parental consent.

8. Cookies & Local Storage

We use:

  • Authentication Cookies: To keep you logged in.
  • Local Storage: To remember your preferences (e.g., dark mode).
  • Analytics: To understand how our app is used.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work.

9. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. When you delete your account:

  • Your profile is deleted immediately.
  • Your videos, comments, likes are removed.
  • Backup copies are deleted within 30 days.

10. Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International Users

ReelHub is operated from India. If you access our app from outside India, you consent to the transfer of your data to India.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

Your continued use of ReelHub after changes means you accept the updated policy.
