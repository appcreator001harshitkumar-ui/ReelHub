Last Updated: 23 September 2026

⚠️ Warning: Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be removed.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your ReelHub account, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • ✅ Your profile (name, username, bio, photo)
  • ✅ All your videos
  • ✅ All your comments
  • ✅ All your likes
  • ✅ All your messages and chats
  • ✅ Your followers and following list
  • ✅ All notifications
  • ✅ Your saved videos and playlists

How to Delete Your Account

1

Open ReelHub App

Open the ReelHub app on your device and make sure you are logged in.

2

Go to Settings

Tap on Profile (bottom navigation) → Tap the ⚙️ Settings button.

3

Tap "Delete Account"

Find the red "Delete Account" button at the bottom of Settings.

4

Confirm Deletion

Read the warning, then tap "Yes, Delete Forever" to confirm.
